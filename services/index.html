Business owners

Key person insurance

Your business’s success often hinges on the vision and contributions of key individuals. Key person insurance helps keep your company financially stable if you lose a vital team member, protecting your operations and long-term growth potential.

Business owners

Buy/sell agreements

Well-thought-out buy/sell agreements are essential to any business partnership. These agreements support a smooth transition of ownership and help reduce disputes, tax burdens, or disruptions if a shareholder exits, passes away, or faces unforeseen circumstances. At Deboski & Co., we can review your agreement to reflect your unique needs and safeguard the legacy of your business.

Business owners

Estate tax planning

Planning for the future includes addressing significant tax liabilities that may arise upon your passing. Insurance-based strategies can help preserve your wealth for those you hold dear, supporting a smoother transfer of assets and reducing financial strain on your estate. Let Deboski & Co. help you craft a plan that supports your growth aspirations, prepares for transitions, and delivers peace of mind as you build your legacy.

Medical and dental professionals

Disability insurance

Your ability to earn is one of your most significant assets. Disability insurance can give you financial security if an injury or illness prevents you from working, allowing you to focus on recovery without compromising your long-term plans.

Medical and dental professionals

Critical illness insurance

Unexpected health events can be both physically and financially draining. Critical illness insurance can provide a safety net: a lump-sum payment to cover treatment costs, support your loved ones, or maintain your financial stability during challenging times.

Medical and dental professionals

Life insurance

Life insurance, when thoughtfully structured, can do more than protect – it can unlock opportunity. Within a corporation, it provides tax-efficient liquidity when it’s needed most. Beyond the immediate protection, well-designed strategies can help enhance long-term value, preserve wealth, and create flexibility for future planning.

Medical and dental professionals

Healthcare spending account

We make it easier for you to stay healthy. A healthcare spending account gives you and your family the flexibility to cover additional medical expenses not included in traditional benefits, empowering you to prioritize your wellness. Let Deboski & Co. help you create a plan that supports both your professional impact and personal well-being.

Legacy-focused families

Legacy mapping

Every legacy begins with a vision. Legacy mapping helps clarify your values, goals, and intentions for future generations, creating a roadmap that helps your story be told and your impact continues.

Legacy-focused families

Wealth transfer strategies

Preserve your hard-earned wealth and pass it on efficiently. Our wealth transfer strategies balance tax efficiency with personal intent, helping your loved ones receive the inheritance you intend with less lost to taxes and administrative complexity.

Legacy-focused families

Donation strategies

For those committed to creating a lasting impact, we design donation strategies that align your philanthropic goals with sustainability and tax advantages. Together, we’ll craft a plan that enhances your giving while benefiting causes close to your heart.
